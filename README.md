# Dino Roar & Explore!

A simple movement game for young children. Pick a dinosaur, listen to its prompt, and try the activity together. A second page lets the dinosaurs show off their dance moves.

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser.
2. Tap or click any dinosaur.
3. Read or listen to the activity, then try the movement.
4. Choose another dinosaur whenever you are ready.

Select **Go to Dino Dance** to open the dance page. Tap any dinosaur there to send it to the dance floor. Tap a different dinosaur to change dancers.

Select **Try Dino Learning** to play three more activities:

- **Dino Sounds:** Listen to a playful synthesized sound and choose the dinosaur that made it. Replay the sound or try another answer.
- **Count the Eggs:** Count up to five eggs and choose the matching number. There is no score, and you can try again at any time.
- **Choose the Story:** Pick what the dinosaurs do next to guide a short, friendly story.

The selected dinosaur wiggles and its activity appears on screen. The browser reads the dinosaur's name and activity aloud when voice is on. Use **Voice on/off** to mute or restore speech, and the repeat button to hear the selected activity again.

## Dinosaur Activities

- **T-Rex:** Stomp your feet 3 times like a giant T-Rex.
- **Triceratops:** Show your horns and shake your head left and right.
- **Pterodactyl:** Flap your arms like wings and fly around the room once.
- **Brachiosaurus:** Reach your hands up high to grab the treetop leaves.
- **Stegosaurus:** Wiggle your bottom and swing your tail.

## Rules

- There is no score, timer, winner, or wrong answer. Play at your own pace.
- Pick any dinosaur and repeat activities as often as you like.
- Follow the movement only if it feels comfortable, and make sure there is room to move safely.
- Voice uses the browser's built-in speech feature. If speech is unavailable, the activity text still works.

## Files

- `index.html` contains the game page and dinosaur choices.
- `style.css` contains the visual design and responsive layout.
- `script.js` contains the activities, selection behavior, animations, and speech controls.
- `dance.html` contains the dinosaur dance page, with four additional dinosaurs.
- `dance.js` handles dinosaur selection and the dance animation.
- `learn.html` contains the sound, counting, and story activities.
- `learn.js` handles sound matching, egg counting, story choices, and game tabs.

The game is static and can be hosted from the repository root with GitHub Pages.
